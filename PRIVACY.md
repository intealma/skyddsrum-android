# Privacy Policy – Shelter Locator

Last updated: 2026-09-29

- **Location** is used only on your device to find the nearest shelters. It is not stored or sent to us.
  When you ask for routes, your position and the shelter positions are sent to the routing services
  (OSRM at routing.openstreetmap.de, and ResRobot/Trafiklab for public transport) to calculate them.
- **Saved places** (Home, Work, School) are stored only on your device.
- **Live status** is read from a public, aggregate-only database (no names). The app does not send personal data to it.
- **Purchases** are handled by RevenueCat and the app store. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app user ID;
  we do not collect your name, email or payment details.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/intealma/skyddsrum-android/issues
